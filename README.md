# Minetale Mod

**Minetale** is a work‑in‑progress open‑source mod that aims to bring the core concepts of *Hytale* to **Minecraft**, preserving the familiar Minecraft aesthetics while introducing new gameplay elements.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Amber wood set** with logs, stripped wood, planks, stairs, slabs, fences, gates, doors, trapdoors, buttons, pressure plates, signs, and hanging signs. Missing Boats.
- **Expanded building blocks** including marble, quartzite, chalk, ledgestone, mossy stone, and volcanic rock, with smooth, cracked, cobbled, brick, chiseled, beam, pillar, stair, slab, and wall variants.
- **Decorative blocks** including Gaia statues, temple benches, and multi-variant marble and quartzite rubble rendered with GeckoLib models.
- **Volcanic ores** for coal, copper, iron, gold, redstone, emerald, lapis, and diamond.
- **Pointed stone variants** for stone, granite, diorite, andesite, deepslate, tuff, calcite, and volcanic rock, with stalactite and stalagmite behavior.
- **Rope blocks** for lightweight, non-colliding decoration. Can be climbed.
- **Plant fibers**, which can drop from leaves and tagged plants and can be composted. Used for rope and stick crafting.
- **Heart of Orbis memory system** that records encounters with supported mobs, shows unlock notifications, persists player progress, and grants experience when memories are claimed through its interface.
- **Forgotten Temple Gateway** that connects the Overworld and the **Edge of the Echo** dimension, including a generated spawn island, return teleportation, cooldowns, and protected gateway-area gameplay.
